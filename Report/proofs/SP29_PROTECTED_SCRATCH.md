## Why the previous mailbox premise needs attention

The earlier late-suffix theorem allowed arbitrary prior work but required
ten raw mailbox inputs to be0. The first simulated tick starts the first
gather: the upper rule's input Age0 loads its Info majority into its
mailboxes. For example a canonical unflagged upper neighbourhood with
Info1, all other private fields0 and Age0 has all ten new mr/ml copies1.
Thus arbitrary initial upper Info does not supply a new global mailbox-
zero premise at first U. A nonconstant suffix normal form does not prove
a physically reachable failure. The exploratory control-dependency probe
retains this distinction and makes no reachability claim.

The replacement argument preserves the specific early scratch values on
which the late result depends. It does not assume all old scratch is0.

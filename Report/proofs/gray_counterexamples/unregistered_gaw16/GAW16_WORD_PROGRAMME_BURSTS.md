GAW2R give selected positive U/2U repair instances. They include full
Word100 Programme, independent History/Mem replacements, repeated

interacting early/late forged packets under two clock phases. Each

changed CA. An actual delayed Programme-to-Out witness shows that its

premise obstruction; the selected operational repair still succeeds.

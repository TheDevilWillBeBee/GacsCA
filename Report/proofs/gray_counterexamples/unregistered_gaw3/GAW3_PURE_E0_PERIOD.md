Second, the two-section SF invariant tolerates this wake and gives the declared changed rule an all-period deterministic theorem for continuing pure original-E0 noise.

## 1. Quantifiers, time convention and decoder

Let F be the fixed total GAW2R rule and beta its frozen emitted immutable ROM. Let E(C,A) be exactly the reset encoding in [GAW2 section6](../unregistered_gaw2/GAW2_THREE_GATHER_PERIOD.md). The upper configuration C may use **all49,037 raw cell bits**, including every GF13/Age20 pattern, invalid head metadata and mutually incoherent SF copies. Lower E has canonical GF, common Age0, Flags/WF0, reset work, correct Programme, and coherent five copies of each logical Info representation word. A is arbitrary coherent unrepresented Info. No recursive intactness or common upper clock is assumed.

An allowed physical error history B is a union of groups, each a singleton or two spatially adjacent sites at one tick. Distinct groups are (24,24)-separated: any points in different groups differ by at least24 in space or at least24 in time. This is the original pure-E0 definition, [Gray pp16–17](../../../../papers_txt/gray_readers_guide.txt). The c104 value is retained for the original classifier; no non-E0 class is used in this theorem. Both B and the underlying configurations may be infinite on the doubly infinite line. Error values may be adaptive, arbitrary legal whole-cell replacements; no independence or probabilistic assumption is used. Immutable rule/ROM constants are not state fields and are not replaced.

Let B_t be the sites of B at row t, with B_{-1}=empty. The physical row X_t is after overwrites at B_t. Computed row Z_{t+1}=F(X_t) is then overwritten at B_{t+1} to give X_{t+1}. A first input may belong to N_{B_0}(E(C,A)): it agrees with that encoding outside B_0. Groups involving B_0 must obey the same global isolation from later groups. Let Y_t be the error-free evolution from the matching E(C,A).

Define Delta5 by reading, at every normal496-word primary and for each native/WORD100 representation bit, the bitwise majority of its five raw Info holders. Native high padding is restored to0 by this vote on the states covered by the theorem. Delta5 agrees with the original noiseless primary decoder on E. The primary-only read can itself be faulty on a currently overwritten holder and is not used as the noisy decoder.

**Theorem.** For every such C,A,B and arbitrary replacements, for every integer n>=0,

    X_0 ∈ N_{B_0}(E(C,A)),
    X_{nU} ∈ N_{B_{nU}}(E(F^n(C),0))    for n>=1,
    Delta5(X_{nU}) = F^n(C).

N_D denotes full-cell agreement outside D. This includes the actual raw reset/boundary fields, not only a corrected vote of selected work variables. It is a restricted deterministic noise theorem; iid noise with positive rate has higher clusters and is not covered.

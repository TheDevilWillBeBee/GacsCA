## Which Gray statement is being tested

Gray's Proposition5 (printed p43, `papers_txt/gray_readers_guide.txt` lines1946–1951) concerns Address, with a real-k allowance of a two-colony level-k spatial box and duration U^k/16. For this real2, that is spatial width2Q² and duration U²/16. The first-period interval [7.5Q,20Q) is far inside such a possible spatial box and appears before that much larger deadline. **It does not contradict Proposition5 even as a transplant to R.** Deriving an actual later orbit or a violation of every permitted box remains open.

The earlier literal all-SF Proposition4 temporary-Mail observations are distinct: this checkpoint targets native Address and does not substitute an all-SF bound for the printed Address-only higher proposition. A finite surviving native island at U does not establish eventual recovery failure, independent-noise unreliability or loss of a remembered bit. Matching Q/U/c is insufficient to attribute R's behavior to Gray's underspecified own programme/layout.

# GI0: an obstruction to the stipulated full-rule interfaces

The unchanged radius-five GT0/GE1 SimBit equations and literal **final
computed primary SimBit** reads cannot be completed into a radius-five
rule by choosing a better fifth programme or auxiliary transitions. The
fixed output projection already requires sites-7 and8. Its smallest
contiguous neighbourhood hull is[-7,8]; its smallest symmetric radius is8.
This is a proved obstruction to the **stipulated interfaces**, not to every
completion of Gray's implicit damaged-state transitions or to Gács's rule.

Q=8192, U=128Q=1048576, lambda24 and c104 are retained. No existing rule,
component, schedule, report or registered checkpoint is changed. GI0 emits
an exact finite **projection**, not an autonomous self-simulating automaton.
The full own beta<=Q, final layout, physical own evaluator and period
composition remain unproved. No intact-history or iid inference follows
from the arbitrary-raw-state witnesses below.

## Exact interface family being ruled out at radius five

Fix the printed native equations, or separately their declared h1 repair.
They compute new GF, Age, Flag1, Flag2 from eleven **old** native words.
Thus there is no recursion through the new Workspace outputs.
Fix GT0's total SimBit equations and p33's mandatory native-dependent wipe:
at holder y, computed local Age0 commits old five-holder Nextbit; at
delivery80Q-1 the two special SimBit groups read the old courier votes;
otherwise SimBit reads the old five-holder SimBit vote. The wipe overrides
all of these when computed Flag1=1 and computed GF differs from raw self GF.
Fix p41's Workspace source as the **final computed** primary SimBit, and
give p33's reader wipe priority for Workspace1. Workspace2 requires
reader computed Flag1=0, so that reader wipe is automatically absent.

Every head, programme, hardwirer, other workspace field and fifth-store
implementation is free in this family, subject to retaining those fixed
equations. In particular no programme assumption appears in the proof.
The current fifth-store result can change new Nextbit; it cannot change
the **old** Nextbit read by new SimBit. The special delivery reads old
courier fields, not their new values. Every other hidden field is absent
from the stipulated source equation. Therefore this fixed output
projection is determined before any choice of a whole beta or auxiliary
transition. One may change those source/priority equations explicitly,
but that is a different interface family.

Gray does not print the complete SimBit rule on all damaged raw states.
The hypothesis that "computed" means the final replacement value is the
same stated interpretation as the frozen order audit. It is consistent
with his explicit native definitions, but is not a separately printed
all-state SimBit evaluation-order definition. Consequently GI0 proves
neither that this is the unique intended Gray completion nor universal
nonexistence of all Gray or Gács implementations. It identifies the exact
interface decision that must be changed or supplied to claim a radius-five
whole construction from these components.

## Emitted total projection and its port independence

The projection reads57 old bits per site: the37 native bits, and five
copies each of SimBit, Nextbit, CourierL and CourierR. These57 are a
projection of the2462-bit component alphabet, not a replacement alphabet.
It emits the37 new native/Workspace bits and five new SimBit bits,42
outputs. Every legal projected raw word has a defined evaluation.

For logical coordinate z define

    V_f(z)=majority{ old holder[z-s].copy[s].f : s in{0,-2,-1,1,2} }.

Let N_y be the actual native result at holder y. For output SimBit copy s
of that holder, put p=(N_y.GF+s) modQ. Before wipe its value is

    V_Nextbit(y+s)                            if N_y.Age=0;
    V_CourierL(y+s)                           if Age=80Q-1 and p=3;
    V_CourierR(y+s)                           if Age=80Q-1 and p=Q-3;
    V_SimBit(y+s)                             otherwise.

Apply the holder's mandatory wipe. The special courier source reduces to
that same logical coordinate because p=3 implies3-N_y.GF=s and p=Q-3
impliesQ-3-N_y.GF=s, for these five copy offsets. These are precisely
GE0's raw target lookup and GT0 scalar routes. In particular the primary
copy s=0 depends on old data at y+[-2,2] and native words at y+[-5,5].
No fifth-programme port or other hidden raw field enters it.

For reader0 with new Address A, the literal Workspace1 source is
P(Q-3-A), where P(y) is this final primary SimBit. Its eligible displacements
are-2..2. Workspace2 reads P(3-A), with displacements-1..3. The reader's
native controls and reader wipe use[-5,5]. Thus all of this projection
uses exactly the interval hull[-7,8] as an upper bound, without assuming
a future complete source transition. This closes the previous audit's
conditional "if the complete source has radius5" premise for the fixed
GT0 source itself.

The source emits finite Boolean graphs with912 declared raw input bits
(16 sites times57 bits) and42 output bits, for both printed and explicit
h1 native versions. They use ordinary bit/word gates, five-holder votes,
the actual native builder, and the fixed old/new equations above. No
oracle, programme lookup, interpreter port or evaluation of the whole
rule occurs. Native results at the six possible source sites are cached;
everything is an acyclic function of old inputs. A direct scalar equation
and actual GE0 source-copy checks independently verify the emitted
projection on retained raw contexts. Finite random checks are recorded as
checks; the all-raw dependency and port-independence conclusions follow
from the explicit equations.

## Why the existing confined eleven-record evaluator cannot be an own beta

The full transition identity must evaluate the rule on arbitrary legal
upper raw neighbours. Extend the site8 pair to full2462-bit upper states
by making every unlisted field equal. The eleven upper raw records-5..5
are identical in A and B, although the required upper Workspace2 output
at0 differs. Therefore any deterministic evaluator whose only variable
raw-rule inputs are these eleven records gives the same result in A and B.
No beta length, loop, word instruction or execution time can repair that
input blindness. This is a programme-independent information obstruction,
not an encoder bit-count measurement.

For the existing physical evaluator the relevant hypothesis is explicit:
its reset/initial packet, immutable beta and native service controls agree;
the moving head remains in its own colony; LOAD uses only the eleven
voted input-record lanes; and no other raw-rule input, signal or tape bank
is provided. At every step its instruction, Address, loaded word and
packet are then identical, by induction. Writes and decoded outputs are
identical. It cannot implement GI0's required full-rule projection on both
upper raw states. This is the stipulated correct-control confined
evaluator interface used by the existing prepared programme proofs.
It is not a theorem about unrestricted head travel, alternative distributed
evaluators, external input banks, or every Gray construction.

Thus enlarging only the CA neighbourhood while keeping GT0's11-lane
gather and LOAD11 input cannot close the own-programme gap. The new rule
would have a dependency its proposed own evaluator cannot read. This
conclusion precedes and is independent of the8192-bit code-fit question.

## Error geometry and reliability steps after changing the range

Original lambda24/c104 location-set definitions are unchanged, as are their
purely geometric conclusions when applicable. Radius8 one-tick cones have
diameter16, and the union for five holders has diameter20, both below24.
Each can meet at most one same-time E0 group. Two-tick cones have diameter32

redundant input preparation, deterministic island confinement, flag-wave
transients and multilevel decoded-noise map need new proofs. Merely keeping

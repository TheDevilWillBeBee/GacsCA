## Exact phase-dependent physical factor

[C batch](gaw15_native.c) includes the frozen GAW9 native equations and
evaluates all actual old GF/Age/F1/F2/WF values. Its pre-WF outputs are
the same native_at outputs of F. It fills each new Programme holder

Old Programme copies in this checkpoint are boolean; the compact bit
track does not cover arbitrary Word100 Programme replacements.

[SF executor](search_gaw15_phase_packets.py) retains separate five-copy
words for every logical y and every SF field, measured against the
actual evolving clean trace. For old words V_h(y), let L_V(y) be their
bitwise 3/5 vote. The computed cell x=y-S[h] has controls

    a = computed Age(x), A = (computed GF(x)+S[h]) mod Q,
    f = computed F1(x), w = f and computed GF(x) != old raw GF(x).

The new holder uses these actual controls, not a centre-cell Address
or common clock. Each clause is obtained directly from frozen local_R:

* Mem resets at stage starts; at early reset and late birth it receives
  the bitwise temporal majority of the three old voted History banks.
* Mail resets, loads voted old Info, or moves voted Mail from y+1/y-1
  according to that holder's computed phase, and f erases it.
* Each History bank resets on its named stage starts and captures the
  named old Info/Mail word on every capture date. Symmetric lanes have
  the same date; **both** captures are performed. No shared-bank tag is
  assumed after a false capture.
* Info at a=0 receives **old voted Out before Out resets**. The 80Q-1
  special stores use the old voted bounded courier; other phases retain
  old voted Info. Couriers use the actual seed/guard/forward/hold rules.
* Head/Out/Scratch reset, birth, execute or hold at their actual phase.
  incoming Head is computed from the old voted packets at y-1,y,y+1;
  execution invokes the unchanged step_packet on each holder's A and
  actual old voted Mem/Programme. SF wipe then zeros all new SF words.

Equal control tuples at one y may reuse this deterministic result.
The vote shortcut when a word appears at least three times is exactly
bitwise majority, not a different decoder. These clause identities
give the physical five-holder factor by induction; no native healing,
packet uniqueness or future silence is a premise.

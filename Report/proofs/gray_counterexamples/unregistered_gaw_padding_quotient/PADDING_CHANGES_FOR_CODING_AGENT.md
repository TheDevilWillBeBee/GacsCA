# Padding changes to consider: proposals only

Nothing below has been applied. Each proposal changes the rule or its
encoding contract and requires a new own-ROM instance. Frozen GAW2R
and G17 remain unchanged. No globally sufficient/minimal fix is claimed.

The [local obstruction proof](PADDING_QUOTIENT_OBSTRUCTION.md) separates
two requirements: legal native upper records for the present period
theorem, or a new decoder relation proved compatible with the actual
emission on padded records. Masking a record in an analysis script does
not establish either requirement.

## Retain the native alphabet: mask normal native commit words

The earlier [J235 proposal](../unregistered_gaw13/GAW13_PADDING_PROPOSAL.md)
specifies the local change. At computed Age0 use the existing service
address a=(computedGF+s) modQ for SF copy s. When a=P+i, i0..5, commit
voted **old** Out with native masks13/20/1/1/1/1; retain Word100 elsewhere
and the final wipe. Update both literal local_transition and emitted
commit_info. Preserve consumption of old Out before resetting it.

With recovered canonical/common-clock lower geometry, all five holders
select the same represented word and mask. Arbitrary old Out then gives
legal coherent normal native words. This addresses GAW17's five illegal
words and earlier high-GF examples for that geometry; clean-shadow
equality and damaged-holder coherence do not follow from the range fact.

The current computed_primary is used only through &1 by Workspace
gates, and every proposed mask preserves bit0. Recheck its complete
new-Info contract anyway, including false Age0 sources, subsequent
courier overrides and final wiping. No new total source/emitter proof
or compilation of this proposal has been performed.

## Permit padded upper inputs: rebuild the projection relation

The source local_transition and computed_primary wipe comparisons would
need to compare computed GF with the13-bit projection of old GF. That
changes padded-input SF behavior at J246's first witness; it is not a
reinterpretation of the frozen equation.

The emitted native acquisition would need central GF/age fallbacks in
registers12/13 masked to13/20 bits in every recipe copy, including the
early entry and all computed-primary source centers. Old GF loads in
workspace/main/source wipe comparisons also need the13-bit projection.
Keep generic Word100 SF/register/Programme/memory loads full-width.
J246's second witness specifically exercises the missing GF fallback
mask. A new total refinement proof must determine and justify every
required mask; passing two selected witnesses is not sufficient.

Python Cell accepting a padded integer does not enlarge the physical
alphabet or prove self-similarity. Declare the finite alphabet/encoding
relation, prove reset/gather/early sources/late evaluation/commit on its
full domain, and handle errors in the added native bits. The present
narrow legal-E theorem cannot be applied to those malformed records.

## Requirements before either proposal becomes a candidate

Freeze the changed source, own beta, ROM and listing; prove their full
refinement and compute worst-case early/late deadlines at the chosen U.
Present beta<=Q and dictionary512 already fail; small masks do not prove
a fit. Re-establish physical-period composition and actual fault
histories with derived future banks/commands, retaining failed runs and
all unmasked old/new records. General real1/higher repair and a positive
iid rate remain separate requirements.

Changing U or c does not alter J246's wipe/fallback predicates. This
algebraic obstruction does not prove that no constants could support
a different repair argument for the unchanged construction.

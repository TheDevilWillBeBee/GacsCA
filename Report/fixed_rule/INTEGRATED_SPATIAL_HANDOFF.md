## Why the output bank was not enough

The earlier isolated evaluator wrote 105 gate outputs into unused memory sinks and read 49 raw outputs at their sources. The integrated stream rule needs **all 154 outputs in its actual Hold cells** so its physical end-of-period `Hold → Info` transition can commit a complete represented upper state. Those 154 Hold sites are distinct, disjoint from all 738 spatial input source sites. One extra output route per wire leaves the maximum static source load at 38 routes, the fixed hardware limit.

Direct rightward Hold routes passed route-capacity checks but finished at tick **34,966**, beyond the 4Q=32,768 evaluator reset. `figs/fixed_rule/spatial_hold_schedule_8q_v1.json` is preserved as the distinguishing failed 4Q attempt; an 8Q window would fit, but would give back the isolated period reduction. For the actual placement, the late gate-output sources are around sites 5,016–5,025 and their Hold targets around 2,863–2,881. Their leftward distances are about 2,150 sites rather than about 6,040 rightward sites.

`spatial_epoch.local_step` now interprets the already allocated two-bit packet `target_gate_slot=3` as leftward mail. A packet with an ordinary gate slot 0–2 moves rightward. No new field, lane, radius, depth selector, or alphabet bit was added. The two traffic phases are separated by encoded launch data: all 19,237 operand packets finish by tick 28,928; all 154 output sources are distinct and emit at tick 28,930, after the final gate at 28,929. Their leftward phases are distinct. The last actual Hold packet arrives at **31,092**, leaving **1,676 ticks** before the 4Q reset. The fixed rule remains radius one and 2,334 bits/cell. Schedule: `figs/fixed_rule/spatial_hold_left_schedule_4q_v1.json`.

The local witness checked 38,782 packet launch/delivery endpoints and all gate/switch events, plus sampled full-ring transitions, through two periods. All 154 Hold outputs decode correctly. `figs/fixed_rule/spatial_hold_left_audit_4q_v1.json` records the event checks. Independent dense replay evolved **65,536 consecutive ticks = 536,870,912 physical site-ticks**, two periods, from the encoded initial cells. ## Literal combined-rule handoff

New `stream28_spatial_overlay.py` defines one fixed radius-seven local transition with the full stream holder state plus the spatial evaluator state: **6,424 physical bits/cell**. Its stage ages are fixed. At old stream Age 82,000,001, a spatial SOURCE cell captures the fivefold-corrected local Data word after the second vote. Starting two ticks after the stage-five reset, the spatial rule evolves for 32,768 ticks; the serial stage-five head is suppressed by the combined local transition. When an OUTPUT site accepts a packet, the local steps at its five physical holder sites each write their corresponding procedure copy. The ordinary stream rule's `INFO` copy at Age U−1 then transfers each Hold word into Info. No evolving host function substitutes for any of these local actions.

A healthy coherent stage-five fixture supplied actual `stream28_holder_rule` fields and the encoded spatial circuit. The literal combined transition captured **all 738 spatial source sites**; checked each of the 154 output words at each of its **five Hold replicas** (770 local steps); then checked all **five Info replicas** for each word at the physical period boundary (770 more local steps). The complete decoded 154-word result also matched an independent scalar upper `stream28_holder_rule.local_step` on the fixture's 15 raw input cells. Source-hashed receipt: `figs/fixed_rule/stream28_spatial_overlay_handoff_v1.json`. This is conditional local composition: the preceding gather/vote stages and intervening stream-holder ticks were initialized coherently for the fixture, not executed as one complete lower work period.

`spatial_codec.py` now encodes and decodes every one of the spatial rule's static and evolving fields, and the combined rule exposes an exact 421-word full-state codec. `spatial_projected.py` removes only fixed per-site evaluator descriptions and retains **all 358 evolving spatial bits**: Age, active gate slot, source/output value, operands, readiness, result, done flag, moving packet, and collision. Together with the existing projected holder state (2,704 bits/105 words), `stream28_spatial_projected.py` defines a **3,062-bit, 119-word** represented state, or 1,785 input words over radius seven. Its diagnostic projected local transition lifts fixed static ROM by address and matches the full combined local transition at a Hold delivery. A deliberate static-route mutation changes the projected result. These are explicit encoding and dependency checks, not self-reference closure.

Current-source regressions passed: 17 spatial local/full-DAG tests, 3 continuous replay tests, and 11 projection, overlay, compiler and topology tests. CPU only; no main-agent GPU job or shared CUDA artifact was touched.

## Exact remaining construction gap

The spatial circuit still evaluates the **old** `stream28_holder_rule` description with digest `ee57d794f4d97875dd7740915079dc70c3e40a3fd88e212c0f4dd225d5257fbc`. The new combined physical rule suppresses the serial head, moves leftward output mail, and changes Data through spatial commits. Its own projected transition is therefore a *different rule*. Neither the old ROM nor the 119-word projection is a description of that changed transition. A closed construction must compile the new projected rule, hard-wire its resulting static evaluator layout by address, and validate that the layout computes the same projected rule it helped define. If the new DAG no longer fits Q8192, three gate slots or 4Q timing, the fixed alphabet/layout and all period evidence must be revised consistently. An opcode inventory or a test ROM cannot settle this fixed-point question.

The next concrete steps are: (1) construct a specialized WordCode description of the 119-word projected combined transition, including bidirectional mail, output latching/commit, stage gating and all holder controller fields; (2) solve the resulting layout/capacity/timing fixed point and test its actual encoded ROM, rather than reuse the old description; (3) run one then two complete lower periods from encoded upper states, with decoded upper transitions, before retiming U or testing repair. The present integrated stream clock remains **U=2^28**, so the 4Q spatial stage alone is not a claim of U≈2^20. Existing Flag2 persistence and computed-SimBit source-fidelity qualifications also remain.

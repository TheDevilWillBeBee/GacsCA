# Next closure and hierarchy obligations after holder recurrence

The implemented fixed holder rule now has two audited one-link work periods,
complete raw controller self-description, fivefold local protection and an
ordinary complete-controller cap orbit. Neither those examples nor recursive
initialization execute a second simulation link. The next work must preserve
that distinction.

## General one-link relation

The current executable certificate is restricted to a right-one/left-zero
forcing profile. To establish a relation for arbitrary represented raw inputs,
prove the following separately and attach distinguishing tests:

1. On canonical lower geometry, fivefold procedure coherence is invariant before
   forcing for arbitrary Data/controller words, and full lower geometry remains
   canonical for arbitrary flags during the mail-free suffix. The physical
   descriptor's complete output, not a supported-opcode inventory, is the target.
2. Each physical instruction phase implements its advertised finite word
   operation. The event scanner's guards must cover FETCH, both operand reads,
   WRITE, LOAD, SEND, metadata lookup/fallback, reflection and rest/reset priority.
   Metadata regeneration must cover all seven records even when represented
   Address changes. The current arbitrary-raw and local-conjugacy tests are
   evidence, not this all-state proof.
3. Every packet route reaches its exact destination without collision and all
   three complete raw histories arrive before 72Q. Existing exact schedule
   bounds and actual fifteen-cell traces support this, but a proof must cover
   arbitrary payloads and every metadata-query path. Payload must not alter a
   supposedly unconditional clock/head bound.
4. After capture and the stage-four reset, the physical suffix is mail-free.
   Candidate-B's unforced Flag1/Flag2 clearing bound must hold for all computed
   flag patterns, not just the certified constant profile. Preserve the literal
   printed-Flag2 counterexample and the explicit candidate-B change. Once flags
   are zero, stage five recomputes the same full raw output and commit copies it
   into every encoded Info field under the same physical rule.

A symbolic instruction transcript is permissible as a proof checker. It must not
become the evolving simulated transition in an experiment. A verifier of the
transcript alone does not prove its refinement by the physical head/mail rule.

## Actual second-link execution

At Q=2^30 and U=2^37, fifteen top cells at depth two require
15*2^60 physical sites and 2^74 physical ticks per top step. Fixed alphabet and
neighborhood do not remove this resource problem. CUDA helps finite batches but
does not by itself close this gap.

A candidate route is exact symbolic/interval compression of **physical** local
histories: many lower colonies differ only in encoded Address/Age or fallback
metadata, and most packet/head movement is free flight. Any such executor needs
certificates that its updates equal the same radius-seven physical descriptor on
all represented cells and times. Branching, carries, ROM boundaries, resets and
noise must split those families or force literal evaluation. No host callback
may simply apply the upper transition once per macroperiod and label that a
second-link run. No extra evaluator register pair or depth-selected ROM/kernel
is available as a shortcut.

A bounded first target is an actual second-link controller event, with complete
raw decoding and a preserved physical dependency certificate; then a full top
macrostep and a second period. The initial cap must use the proven backup-head
pulse orbit if chosen; its algebraic consistency is not organized termination or
fault-tolerant amplification.

## Repair/noise next

The two-cell, two-tick repair result applies to tested coherent zero-flag states.
A single physical Flag1 deletion at a moving forcing front gives a one-site
phase delay after each of eight literal steps. All procedure fields agree.
The all-front recurrence proves rejoining within 178,956,970 ticks for this
specific mail-free example. This does not bound an arbitrary fault island.

Next examine mail while geometry/flags are damaged: physical Flag1 erasure can
remove a packet even when the corrected computation words are locally sound.
Measure survival/recovery of decoded raw controller fields over whole periods,
not just persistence of Data. Only then attach independent noise at a specified
physical space-time rate, preserve its realization and compare recovery scaling.

# Superseded fixed-rule attempts

This tree preserves earlier fixed-rule research in its original relative
source/report/test/experiment layout. Most candidates evaluated their own
transition with serial instruction streams and had large work-period costs.
The archive also includes rejected layouts, older spatial timing proposals,
and the full pre-cleanup status history. Their measurements remain useful as
counterexamples and design evidence; they are not the current Q8192/U2^20
candidate.

The active construction and focused checks remain in the repository's
`gacsca/fixed_rule`, `tests/fixed_rule`, `experiments/fixed_rule`, and
`Report/fixed_rule` directories. Some predecessor modules stay active because
the current implementation imports them. To execute an archived driver with
its original imports, use the complete pre-cleanup checkpoint `bae5fd2` in a
separate checkout. Do not infer a completed self-simulation claim from any
historical candidate report.

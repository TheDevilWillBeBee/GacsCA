## Consequence for the construction

[RESIDUAL_NOISE_DOMAIN.md](RESIDUAL_NOISE_DOMAIN.md) proved separation under
canonical Address and exhibited a one-bit artificial counterexample outside
that domain. This continuation closes the distinction between that artificial
value and the actual even-valued residuals: those actual words can also influence
a raw controller when fresh geometry damage occurs. The influence here is
transient and is erased physically along with the residue. No upper decoded
failure or complete new-fault repair is established.

Future repeated-noise analysis must retain these words and their transient reads,
or justify their physical erasure in a revised fixed rule with self-description
and macrostep closure checked again. The immediate next obligation is to follow
the common state carrying the159 fresh-damage differences to a verified reset or
repair interface; paired coupling alone cannot supply that conclusion. General
amplification/thresholds, robust finite caps, depth three, Q/U optimization and
Flag2/SimBit source issues remain open. The full project goal stays active.

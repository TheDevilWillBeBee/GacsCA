# Physical defects and executed encoded-controller repair

2026-09-26. The new CPU exception layer retains complete projected physical
defects and evolves them with literal local G. A targeted experiment has passed
its key repair and rejoin stages: six physical bit errors produce two persistent
wrong encoded controller operands; actual self-evaluation repairs the represented
transition; the physical worlds rejoin after the next real scratch reset.
Both work periods and the independent audit passed. Total run time was
947.670568 s, with 86768 KiB peak RSS.

The physical rule and ROM are unchanged. This is correction across one simulation
link, not a full upper work period, stochastic threshold or amplification theorem.
The 15-colony fixture has distinct represented neighborhood positions and an
active upper controller whose next state depends on the corrupted operand.

## Fixed physical semantics and state representation

The fixed projected physical rule is G=pi F iota, with the same Address-dependent
ROM hard-wiring used throughout the construction. Every literal defect update
evaluates the complete native F on 15 lifted physical neighbors, projects its
105 mutable words, and regenerates metadata from the resulting Address. This
distinction matters for faulty geometry: mutable Address is not silently treated
as canonical, and stale metadata is not carried as extra physical state.
On the canonical background, F and lifted G agree by the existing identities.

Q=32768, U=2147483648, radius seven, 105 mutable words/2704 bits and 154 lifted
words/4090 bits remain fixed independently of depth. Descriptor
`6e2f29f61bf281ec5d346cd58e717cf7e5b0afea263d2c17a18a58de295d8d23` and ROM
`4dc026b976c541001421dd214df9c9e33f6f851053d4ba5f53dbbec925ba5645` are unchanged.
The source interpretation remains Gray pp. 31–32's ProgramBit projection and
Gacs §§9.2–9.3's identical/suitably modified rule. The candidate-B Flag2 and D10
choices and prior source ambiguities remain explicit; this experiment does not
establish the complete papers' correction/amplification hypotheses.

`retimed_holder_cpu_faults.World` combines the general-context background with
a dictionary of complete projected physical exceptions. While exceptions exist,
the affected outputs are exactly the radius-seven expansion of their sites.
Those outputs are evaluated synchronously from old raw neighborhoods. Outside
that set, equality with the background follows from locality. Exceptions are
removed only when all 105 mutable words equal the new background cell. Raw
controller, mail, flags, Wf and geometry are retained, including malformed
combinations. Default storage limits are 1024 exceptions and 4096 candidate
outputs; these are executor resource limits, not physical alphabet limits.

Capacity failures occur before publication or restore the saved background and
exception state. A literal-tick budget can halt execution at an exact retained
state; it does not resample, truncate faults or silently use a healthy result.
When no exceptions remain, the same physical background executor advances to
actual clock boundaries. No upper transition evolves this state.

### Rebase is not correction

Three bad lower copies can make an incorrect Data word coherent in all five
holders. `absorb_data` moves such an actual value into the background only after
checking equality of all five copies. It retains every other exceptional raw
field and checks exact same-time equality before/after the representation change.
The wrong value stays wrong. Unaffected physical holders cannot change.
This permits subsequent actual computation on damaged Info without taking
billions of literal exception steps. Empty exceptions mean representability,
not recovery; the experiment explicitly checks that the decoded operands remain
wrong after this rebase.

## Complete-descriptor replica-repair argument

`certify_retimed_holder_replica_repair.py` identifies majority-gate expressions
in the actual complete F descriptor by exact structural interning of its word
operations. It finds 119 reached five-copy majority gates and traverses all
154 output dependency graphs, stopping at those gates. No raw procedure input
remains reachable outside this cut. The gate implementations are checked over
all 32 Boolean input assignments. The 64-bit gate is bit-local NAND logic, so
the per-bit argument covers arbitrary words; the one-bit gate's arithmetic
threshold is checked exactly. Both variants also pass all 80 assignments for
two arbitrarily corrupted copies of a coherent value. Identifying Boolean gates
with threshold two instead of three fails the complete-output dependency cut.

Consequently, if nonprocedure inputs are unchanged and each reached corrected
procedure word is unchanged, all complete F outputs agree. In particular, up to
two changed copies per initially coherent logical procedure field preserve the
next full output, including every controller word. Projection gives the same G
result. No clock, head-count or canonical-geometry assumption is needed for this
one-step comparison; geometry/Signal/Wf themselves must remain unchanged between
the compared inputs. The certificate passed in 0.207937 s at 38028 KiB peak RSS.
It does not cover three-copy errors or general geometry recovery.

## Executed repair scenario

The top fixture has 15 represented cells, varied Data and a READ_B controller at
cell 7. Its `rb` operand is actually used. The experiment changes two encoded
copies of that operand, in upper holders 6 and 8. For each encoded word, three
lower Data replicas are flipped by one bit: six physical field-bit changes in
total. This exceeds the lower two-copy repair contract while staying within the
upper contract.

1. Literal lower step zero-to-one spreads each wrong Data value coherently.
   Exactly two actual MEM words are rebased; decoded Info remains the wrong
   represented configuration. The complete initial defect cone is saved.
2. Actual gathers and the full evaluator operate on those wrong encoded inputs.
   At capture time, all raw Hold words equal the healthy upper transition while
   Info still contains the wrong old operands. The upper rule has performed its
   own five-copy correction during execution.
3. At WF_START+100, two lower physical holders around a live evaluator head have
   every procedure field replaced by seeded random values. Literal local G
   repairs that later pulse in one tick. The complete before/faulty/after cone
   is saved; geometry, Signal and Wf are unchanged by this injection.
4. Real commit writes repaired Hold into Info. Full E validation checks every
   lower physical site. Wrong scratch histories still distinguish the damaged
   world from the independently evolved healthy physical reference.
5. The actual next reset clears that scratch. At time U+1, every stored physical
   background field and the empty exception sets agree. No reference state was
   installed. The second full physical period continues from the retained state.

During physical evolution, diagnostic scalar upper transitions and word-program
evaluation are patched to raise if called. Native local physical transitions,
fixed ROM lookup, actual packet/controller execution and diagnostics outside
those intervals remain allowed. The healthy reference evolves separately and
is used only in comparisons.

### Failed control retained

The first proposed three-colony run stopped before evolution: changing three
upper operand copies did not change its result. Inspection showed that geometry
maintenance cleared the affected upper controller on that very short ring.
That fixture could not distinguish operand repair from clearing. The current
15-colony fixture repairs two bad operand copies, while three change 15 raw
output words (phase, rb and value replicas). The active controller survives and
performs a READ_B-to-WRITE transition. The three-copy negative control is a
diagnostic upper-rule calculation, not a separate physical work-period run. The failed source and log remain preserved;
the acceptance criterion was retained rather than weakened.

This also refines the interpretation of the earlier three-colony general-context
result: it correctly exercised physical flags and full periods, but its first
upper controller changes were clearing. It is not used as evidence of active
operand repair here.

## Terminal execution and independent audit

Both physical periods passed; all 491520 physical cells satisfy E at each
commit. The run took 947.670568 s, including the independently evolved healthy
physical reference and full streaming validation, at 86768 KiB peak RSS (84.7
MiB). It reached the repaired Hold stage after 153.929750 s and full physical
rejoin at U+1 after 488.284185 s. Exception evolution required two literal ticks
and 51 complete local F evaluations. Exactly two persistent wrong Data words
were rebased after the initial fault; no healthy values were inserted.

The independent audit passed in 1.510689 s, 78116 KiB peak RSS. It recomputed all
34 affected raw outputs of the initial defect cone and found 10 differences from
the unperturbed output, confirming that the encoded faults survived local repair.
All 17 affected outputs of the late two-holder pulse match its unperturbed
transition. These are complete causal cones, so equality outside them follows
from locality. Every local comparison checks scalar F against its word
expression, then applies the fixed projection.

At both commits, all 2310 normalized raw Info words match the independently
computed clean upper trajectory. Seventy controller words change in the first
simulated tick and 47 in the second. The three-copy diagnostic control differs,
so success cannot be attributed to irrelevant operand changes or geometry
clearing. Saved Data at physical rejoin equals the separately evolved healthy
Data; the execution additionally compared all controller/mail/flag/Signal arrays.
Final flags, controller state and mail are empty. All runs are terminal.

The index `figs/fixed_rule/retimed_holder_cpu_encoded_repair_evidence_v1.json`
binds the three passing manifests, raw snapshot, ten passing tests, both retained
failed attempts, and prior general-context/period evidence. Recorded source and
artifact hashes were checked before freezing the index.

## Tests, audit and resources

Six defect tests passed in 4.383 s: one-bit Info repair, two damaged procedure
holders during active writing, exact two-step projected evolution of arbitrary
geometry/controller/Signal defects at clock boundaries, persistent three-copy
corruption, state-preserving rebase, locality, and atomic resource/ownership
guards. The arbitrary-geometry tests establish exact evolution, not a general
recovery bound. Four audit tests passed in 1.835 s; they reject absent faults,
fabricated healthy output, missing causal halo and non-atomic post-step capacity
overflow.

The first defect-test attempt supplied incoherent healthy capture buffers and
was correctly rejected by the background's Signal guard. Its exact source/log
are retained. Correcting those buffers preserved the arbitrary fault draws and
required no implementation change.

The independent audit reads saved raw snapshots, checks scalar F against its
word descriptor, applies the fixed projection, and recomputes complete affected
output cones. It separately checks persistent initial corruption and complete
one-tick repair of the later pulse. It compares both macrosteps, repaired Hold,
all Info words, final flags/controllers, and saved Data at physical rejoin.
Full physical rejoin additionally uses the execution-time comparison of all
background arrays; only Data is saved for both worlds at that instant.

All commands use `ulimit -v 524288` and `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1`. Builds and data
are private to the fixed_rule namespace. No shared source, GPU job, CUDA artifact
or historical dataset was changed. Substantial GPU scheduling remains with the
main agent; the separate reservation remains pending and unused.
